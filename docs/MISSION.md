# aiconsumerrights.org: mission and values

This is what the site holds itself to. If the site falls short of it, please
[tell us what's wrong, why, and how to fix it](mailto:feedback@aiconsumerrights.org),
or [open an issue](https://github.com/e-allora/aiconsumerrights.org/issues).

## Mission

AI now helps make decisions about people: loans, jobs, prices, what we see.
Most people aren't told how, or what they can do about it. This site explains
your rights in plain words, in five languages, and gives people a calm place
to say what should change, across the US, Europe, Brazil, and beyond.

## How we work

- **Critique ideas, never people.** No person, company, or group is shamed here.
- **Assume good faith.** People in every sector are mostly doing their best with what they know.
- **Ethics and transparency.** We name our sources, our tools, and our AI help, and we say what isn't finished.
- **Positivity with substance.** Kind and rigorous at the same time.
- **Lift while you climb.** Everyone is welcome as they are. Beginner questions count.
- **Protect privacy.** Collect as little as possible, and say plainly what we keep.
- **Accept correction gracefully.** Criticism with a reason makes this site better.

## Never allowed

Hate, harassment, doxxing, slurs, or mocking anyone's question.

*Bigotry is not a perspective. Cruelty is not honesty. Debate is welcome;
dehumanization never is.*
